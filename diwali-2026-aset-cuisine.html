🍛 Do you need to check out some South Asian Cuisine?

Explore Indian and South Asian flavors around Toledo, Ann Arbor, and Metro Detroit. These independent restaurants are suggestions for further exploration, not event caterers.

📍 Toledo, Ohio

  • Bombay Kitchen — Indian street food and curries; 5228 Monroe St, Toledo.
  • Indian Jewel of Toledo — biryani, tandoori dishes, and vegetarian options; 7406 Central Ave, Toledo.
  • Tandoor Cuisine of India — North Indian favorites; 2247 S Reynolds Rd, Toledo.

📍 Ann Arbor, Michigan

  • Madras Masala — South Indian and Indo-Chinese cuisine; 2016 Packard St, Ann Arbor.
  • Cardamom — fresh Indian cooking and house-roasted spices; 1739 Plymouth Rd, Ann Arbor.
  • Namaste Flavours — dosa, biryani, and curries; 400 S Division St, Ann Arbor.

📍 Detroit & Metro Detroit, Michigan

  • Charminar Biryani House — biryani and Indian classics; 1226 Griswold St, Detroit.
  • The Coriander — Indian, Bangladeshi, and Nepali-inspired menu; 22542 Telegraph Rd, Southfield (Metro Detroit).
